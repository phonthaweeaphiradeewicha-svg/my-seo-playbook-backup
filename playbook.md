## Where Experts Disagree
**Disagreement 1**
On YouTube, Julian Goldie explained how AI is useful for himself and other organizations, especially in AI SEO. AI could support in showing helpful & convenient contents, meta descriptions & link generation, uncovering low-competition & high relevance keywords by being the personal consultant, generating journalistic contents, and ranking across various search engines (e.g., Google Search, Google AI, Google Overviews, Google AI Mode, and ChatGPT recommendations). 
(Source: Julian Goldie, YouTube, June 2026 —
https://youtu.be/8NAjD6HcHzk)

On the contrary, Eli Schwartz, the digital growth advisor explained that AI SEO trends could be temporary, similar to the past spam tactics like spun content plugins until search engines issue updates arrived like Google's Panda in 2011. From Eli Schwartz's point of view, long-term search optimization requires creating content that truly helps human readers rather than writing for crawlers or LLMs.
(Source: Eli Schwartz, LinkedIn, 2026 —https://www.linkedin.com/posts/schwartze_many-think-ai-content-is-the-new-silver-bullet-share-7456620033339904000-wy_o/)

**Disagreement 2**
Ross Simmonds developed a GEO strategy to let AI models to source and surface the brand when answering user queries. Based on Princeton/Georgia tech study, adding citations, relevant quotations, and statistics can increase the source visibility by up to 40% in generative engine responses, so simple keyword is ineffective. Because LLMs train on community discussions, distributing content across channels like Reddit, Quora, and social media is critical for influencing AI output, so posting helpful information on public websites is important.
(Source: Ross Simmonds, LinkedIn, 2026 —
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/whats-generative-engine-optimization-geo-how-do-ross-simmonds-wol8c/)

On the LinkedIn post, Lily Ray indicated that many top-cited brands in LLM responses earn citations because they are already leading, widely recognized brands like Levi, represented in historical training data. Another point is that most visibility in Google AI Overviews and AI Mode stems from solid traditional SEO and brand equity built over time, not so called "GEO magic", so everyone must be wary of marketing agencies that guarantee AI search visibility or claiming that they have secret formulas.
(Source: Lily Ray, LinkedIn, 2026 —
https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/another-way-youre-being-lied-geo-ai-search-lily-ray-t8shf/)
